Three questions before shipment

The machine is the Indian side. The buyer’s wall is the other side. A transformer changes voltage only.

1. Connector

Earth and current

Type D or Type M can seat in a matching earthed socket. Type C cannot supply a machine. Every other country needs its own earthed plug, an IEC 60309 connector, or a hard-wired junction.

2. Voltage

15 V band

220–240 V matches a 230 V machine. A 100–127 V site needs a continuous-duty step-up transformer rated to at least 150% of peak wattage, or a machine built for that voltage.

3. Frequency

Motor speed

India is 50 Hz. On 60 Hz a synchronous motor runs about 20% faster. Specify a 60 Hz motor or a variable-frequency drive. A supply marked 50/60 Hz is unaffected.
