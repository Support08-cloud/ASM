Critical distinction

Adapter and transformer

A pin adapter changes the shape of the contact. It does not change voltage or frequency. That is the usual cause of a burnt supply on imported equipment.

Physical fit only

Pass-through plug adapter

  • Changes the pin layout so the plug can enter the local socket.
  • Does not convert voltage. 110 V equipment on a 230 V socket can fail immediately.
  • Safe where the plate reads 100–240 V, 50/60 Hz, as on a switch-mode supply.

Voltage conversion

Step-up or step-down transformer

  • Changes 220–240 V to 110–120 V, or the reverse, for a single-voltage device.
  • Size it for continuous duty, with 150% of peak wattage as headroom.
  • Does not change 50 Hz to 60 Hz. A synchronous motor will change speed.

Contract clause

ELECTRICAL COMPLIANCE AND DELIVERY

1. The purchaser acknowledges that the equipment operates at the voltage (V), frequency (Hz), and current (A) stated on its rating plate.

2. Before installation or the first power-on, the purchaser shall confirm that the facility supply matches that rating plate.

3. A non-earthed travel adapter, or any passive pin converter used above its current rating, voids the manufacturer warranty. Where a step-up or step-down transformer is required, it shall be rated for at least 150% of peak operating wattage.

4. Damage from the wrong voltage, the wrong line frequency, or a missing protective earth remains the purchaser’s liability.
